## Research checklist (per company update)
1. Source document ingested and content-hashed (`source_document`).
2. Facts extracted and mapped to canonical taxonomy (`financial_fact`), scope/period/unit set.
3. Reconciliation checks pass (balance sheet balances, EPS ties to share count).
4. Ratios computed via versioned formula engine (`ratio_definition` / `ratio_value`).
5. Narrative/thesis drafted with evidence links (`evidence_link`) to every claim.
6. Peer review — a second person checks source links and narrative before publish.
7. Correction policy: a correction creates a new version; it never silently overwrites history.
